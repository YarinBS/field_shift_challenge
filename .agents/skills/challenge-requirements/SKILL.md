# Challenge requirements

Retrieve and inspect the official challenge description and distinguish its requirements from
the project's own product ideas. Map each requirement to verified implementation evidence,
partial coverage, or a gap. Identify value beyond the minimum and recommend improvements
that fit hackathon feasibility.

Cite official sources and repository locations. Mark inaccessible or unverified requirements
as unresolved rather than guessing. Do not modify the product or roadmap unless explicitly
requested.

## Success criteria

The output is a requirement-by-requirement comparison with evidence, gaps, priorities,
feasible recommendations, and a clear separation between official and local scope.
