# Research data

Prioritize official NASA and authoritative agricultural sources relevant to the farm's
geographic location. For each candidate, verify official documentation and record:
spatial and temporal resolution, variables, coverage, format, access requirements, licensing,
provenance, limitations, and how it could support crop suitability or rotation recommendations.
Compare alternatives and identify validation gaps.

Do not download large datasets without explicit approval. Do not present an unverified source
or scientific inference as fact. Update `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md` and `docs/DATASETS.md` only
after findings and verification status are documented.

## Success criteria

The result has traceable official references, decision-relevant comparisons, explicit limits,
and accurately updated dataset registries. If research cannot verify a claim, say so.
